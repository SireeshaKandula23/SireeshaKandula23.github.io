# Mission

## Purpose

Build a public, professional portfolio that presents Sireesha Kandula's verified QA engineering experience and capabilities to hiring teams seeking SDET, QA automation, test automation, Salesforce QA, and API testing talent. The portfolio also supports professional networking and freelance credibility.

## Audience and Value

Recruiters and engineering leaders should be able to quickly understand Sireesha's quality engineering strengths, review relevant experience and skills, inspect approved work, download a resume, and reach out through professional channels. The site should be clear, credible, accessible, and easy to use on mobile and desktop.

## Version 1 Scope

- Present a concise professional introduction and career-focused About section.
- Describe verified experience and skills in a scannable format.
- Provide a Work foundation without inventing projects or results.
- Make the resume viewable and downloadable.
- Provide professional contact paths through email, LinkedIn, and GitHub.
- Support responsive layouts, accessibility, search visibility, and static GitHub Pages hosting.

## Content and Privacy Guardrails

- Publish only accurate, verifiable professional claims; do not fabricate metrics, projects, or outcomes.
- Exclude confidential employer or customer information, credentials, internal URLs, and unapproved assets.
- Do not publish a phone number, personal location, or family and hobby details.
- Do not add user accounts, a backend contact form, or unnecessary collection of visitor data.

## Out of Scope for Version 1

Authentication, a database or CMS, server-side application logic, payments, a blog, and a dedicated freelance-services section. Real case studies may be added when approved, publishable artifacts exist.

## Success Criteria

A visitor can identify Sireesha's role and strengths, review relevant experience and skills, access the resume, and find professional contact links without encountering fabricated or private information. The site remains usable with keyboard navigation and across supported screen sizes.
